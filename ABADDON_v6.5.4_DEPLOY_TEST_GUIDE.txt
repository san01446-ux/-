ABADDON v6.5.4 Deployment & Test Guide

Deployment
1. Back up the currently deployed project.
2. For a complete replacement, upload the contents of the FULL_BOT ZIP.
3. For an update from v6.5.3, overwrite with the PATCH_ONLY ZIP.
4. Restart the Render service once after the files are uploaded.
